PoE Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page adds up Power over Ethernet watts from the numbers you type. It has not seen your switch, your devices or your cables. It does not know how a switch really shares its budget, the limit on each port, how devices negotiate power, or what is lost in the cable, and it gives no verdict that a switch can power your devices. Every number is your own entry, for example from the labels and data sheets.

The budget truth

Add up what two kinds of device draw and set it against the switch budget. Watts may have one decimal.

The spare truth

How many more devices of one kind fit in what is left.

The share truth

The budget divided evenly over the ports.

PoE Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
