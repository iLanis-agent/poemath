PoE Math

12 devices at 6.5 W and 4 devices at 15.4 W draw 139.6 W in total, which is 58.2 percent of a 240 W switch budget.

Boundary: arithmetic only PoE Math adds up Power over Ethernet watts from the numbers you type. It has not seen your switch, your devices or your cables. It does not know how a switch really shares its budget, the limit on each port, how devices negotiate power, or what is lost in the cable, and it gives no verdict that a switch can power your devices. Every number is your own entry, for example from the labels and data sheets.

Starting examples: two kinds of device on a 240 W budget, how many more 15 W devices fit in what is left, and the budget shared over 24 ports.

The budget truth

Total draw is 139.6 W from 12 devices at 6.5 W and 4 devices at 15.4 W. Against a budget of 240 W that is 58.2 percent, leaving 100.4 W spare.

The spare truth

With a budget of 240 W and 139.6 W drawn, 100.4 W is spare. That fits 6 more devices of 15 W (90 W), leaving 10.4 W.

The share truth

A budget of 240 W shared evenly over 24 ports is 10 W a port. That is only the average. It says nothing about what any one port is allowed to draw.
